# CLAUDE.md: SHVR tutorial videos

Read `standard/STANDARD.md` before making or changing any tutorial, and follow the README's "Make a new tutorial" steps.
Non-negotiables:
- Blur every customer name, address, phone, email and staff or crew name, and any dollar amount the task doesn't need. Record it in `privacy_notes`.
- Screenshots only. Never use a live store's saved data, exports or logins.
- Nothing goes to a franchise owner until Jason has watched it (`review.jason_watched`).
- One task per video, 60 fps, 16:9 and 9:16, narration voice "Sarah", -16 LUFS, no em dashes, no dollar figures in text.
- Every heading in title case (first letter of each word capitalized); the renderer applies it.
- Run `python3 standard/gates.py tutorials/<topic>` before showing anyone.
